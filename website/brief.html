01
Genesis & Opportunity

What Konjo Is

A two-sided marketplace app tailored for Addis Ababa. Vetted beauty and wellness professionals (barbers, nail technicians, braiders, makeup artists, massage therapists) list themselves; clients book them to their home, hotel or office, pay seamlessly in-app, and rate the service.

👑

Founders & Backing

The business is owned and operated by two passionate founders, Tati and Sara. Addis Alemayehou is funding the build, tech infrastructure, and early go-to-market costs.

📍

Strategic Geographies

Laser-focused on high-density, high-trust corridors in Addis Ababa: Bole, Old Airport, CMC, and Ayat. Capturing expats, returning diaspora, and local corporate professionals.

🎓

Addis Coders Partnership

A student team designs and builds the complete product under Jelani’s direct technical supervision as a paid, milestone-based project. Konjo owns 100% of the code, data and IP; students gain a real, commercial product on their CVs + stipends.

System Architecture

The Three Surfaces to Build

The product ecosystem consists of three interconnected surfaces engineered for distinct users: clients seeking effortless luxury care, professionals managing their craft and earnings, and administrators safeguarding quality and escrow.

Primary Consumer Interface

Client App Android + Mobile Web

Engineered for frictionless discovery, upfront transparency, and calm confidence when welcoming a therapist into one's private sanctuary.

  • Phone OTP Sign-up: Simple, passwordless authentication using Ethiopian mobile numbers with SMS OTP verification.

  • Verified Pro Profiles: High-resolution portfolios, certified skill badges, transparent pricing, customer ratings, verified reviews, and live availability.
  • Dynamic Zone-Based Booking: Select date, time slot, and precise destination address with auto-calculated zone travel fees.
  • Ethiopian Payment Stack: Seamless in-app checkout via Telebirr, CBE Birr, or card (Chapa gateway for diaspora/USD cards); cash flag available for pilot testing.
  • Lifecycle Management: Real-time booking status tracker, 1-click rescheduling, and clear cancellation rules.
  • Dual-Dimension Rating: Separate ratings for Technique (1-5★) and Professionalism (1-5★) plus written feedback.
  • Rebooking & History: 1-tap rebooking of favourite professionals, itemized receipts, and booking archive.

03
Operating Mechanics

Core Flows the Product Must Get Right

Three critical flows form the foundation of Konjo's trust, reliability, and business model. These require rigorous state management and zero-defect execution.

Flow 01 · State Machine

The Complete Booking Lifecycle

Deterministic 6-stage lifecycle engineered to guarantee service fulfillment and prevent abandoned appointments.

1

Client Books

Client picks pro, time & address. Funds locked in Konjo escrow via Telebirr, CBE Birr or card.

2

Pro Accepts

Push notification alerts pro with 15-minute countdown. If no acceptance, auto-reassigns to next nearest vetted pro.

3

Arrival & Check-In

Pro commences journey (travel fee forfeited if client cancels now). Upon arrival, pro checks in.

4

Service & Timer

Pro delivers treatment. Real-time session timer displays on both apps. Dual SOS monitoring active.

5

Check-Out & Rating

Pro checks out. Client rates technique (1-5★) & professionalism (1-5★). Receipt issued.

6

Escrow Release

Konjo deducts 18% commission. Remaining 82% + travel fee queued for pro's weekly Friday payout.

Stage 1 of 6: Client Books
Flow 02 · Financial Engine

Escrow, Commission & Cancellation Economics

Client pays at booking. Funds are held by Konjo, not the professional, until check-out. Commission (18%) is deducted; pro’s share is settled weekly.

Flow 03 · Trust & Safety

Trust & Safety: Built for Peace of Mind

Because personal care takes place in private homes, hotels, and offices, safety is non-negotiable. Every feature was designed to reassure both clients and professionals.

🏛️

Admin Vetting Gate

No professional goes live autonomously. Tati & Sara personally review National Fayda IDs, inspect background certificates, and review portfolios before granting active marketplace status.

🪪

First-Massage Fayda ID Gate

Clients verify their phone number on sign-up. For their first in-home massage booking, clients must verify their National Fayda ID to protect therapists against vulnerability.

🌸

Female-Therapist-Only Option

Clients can enforce a strict preference for female professionals. The backend matching algorithm filters exclusively for verified female therapists during initial booking and reassignment.

⏱️

Live Session Timer

During all visits, a synchronized session timer runs on both devices. Eliminates billing ambiguity and provides real-time tracking of therapist arrival and completion.

🚨

Dual SOS Emergency Dispatch

An omnipresent SOS button on both client and professional screens. One tap immediately alerts the Konjo admin console, sends emergency SMS alerts, and broadcasts exact live GPS coordinates.

⭐

3.0★ Auto-Hide Guardrail

If a professional’s cumulative rating dips below 3.0 out of 5.0, their profile is instantly hidden from search results, pending an immediate formal review and interview by the founders.

04
Engineering Specifications

Technical Guidance

Recommended architecture tailored for Addis Ababa's mobile network conditions, local payment rails, and high-performance cross-platform delivery.

Mobile Surfaces (Client & Pro)

React Native (Expo)

Single cross-platform TypeScript codebase for the Android apps. Enables rapid iteration, OTA updates, and simple future compilation to iOS.

  • Expo SDK 52+
  • React Native
  • TypeScript
  • Offline-First State
Web Surfaces (Admin & Mobile Web)

Next.js (React)

Server-rendered web experience for the desktop admin panel and responsive mobile web booking portal for diaspora clients without the Android app.

  • Next.js / React
  • Tailwind CSS
  • Server Actions
  • Role-Based Access
Backend Services & Queues

Node.js (NestJS) or Python (FastAPI)

Clean, modular REST API with asynchronous queue workers for 15-minute assignment countdowns, escrow timers, and SMS/push dispatches.

  • Node.js / FastAPI
  • PostgreSQL
  • Redis (BullMQ / Celery)
  • Docker

Mandatory External Integrations

🇪🇹 Telebirr & CBE Birr

Direct merchant integration for native mobile money payments and weekly batch payouts in Ethiopian Birr (ETB).

💳 Chapa Gateway

Payment rail supporting international cards (Visa/Mastercard) in USD for diaspora visitors, plus local debit settlement.

💬 Africa's Talking / Ethio Telecom SMS

High-priority SMS gateway for instant OTP phone verification, booking reminders, and emergency SOS alerts.

🗺️ Google Maps Platform

Reverse geocoding, zone polygon boundary checks (Bole, Old Airport, CMC, Ayat), and travel fee calculations.

🔔 Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)

Sub-second push notifications for pro job offers, client tracking updates, and appointment status alerts.

🔒 Security & Data Privacy

  • Encryption at Rest: All sensitive PII (Fayda IDs, addresses, phone numbers) encrypted in PostgreSQL.
  • Zero ID Client Visibility: Professional and client government IDs are strictly server-only for admin review; never rendered to peers.
  • Zero Card Storage: Payment credentials tokenized through Chapa/Telebirr; never stored on Konjo servers.
  • Anti-Abuse Protections: Rate-limiting on SMS OTP endpoints to eliminate SMS pumping attacks.
  • Documented Deletion Procedure: Compliant account deletion and PII sanitization procedures documented in code.

⚡ Performance & Local Conditions

  • Mid-Range Android Optimization: Smooth 60fps performance on budget Android chipsets common in Addis Ababa.
  • Slow 4G / 3G Resilience: Aggressive asset compression, optimistic UI states, and request retry mechanisms.
  • Offline-Tolerant Booking: Drafts, addresses, and selections preserved locally across transient connection drops.
  • Single i18n Dictionary: Unified Amharic & English locale file maintained from Day 1 for immediate bilingual parity.
  • Lean App Bundle Size: Strict tree-shaking keeping APK downloads small for low-bandwidth mobile downloads.

A disciplined 10-week sprint leading to a public launch in November 2026, aligned with the peak diaspora return for Genna (Ethiopian Christmas).
