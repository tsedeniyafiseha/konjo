## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the sensory calm and ancient botanical heritage of Ethiopian wellness rituals, reinterpreted for a contemporary luxury audience. The atmosphere is quiet, editorial, and deliberately unhurried—evoking a private sanctuary, bespoke salon suite, or artisanal apothecary. 

The emotional tone balances grounding earthly tranquility with pristine modern craftsmanship. It relies heavily on generous negative space, soft ambient illumination, and precise proportions rather than decorative clutter. The aesthetic direction blends **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with **Tactile Botanical Materiality**: flat surfaces are softened through delicate, warm translucent tints, ultra-fine hairline divisions, and subtle ambient shadows that ground white content cards over airy linen canvas backdrops.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from native botanical flora and highland earth tones:

- **Primary (`#9CAF88` - Sage Mist)**: Used for primary brand moments, active states, serene highlight badges, and primary buttons. Its deep shade counterpart (`#586851` - Olive Cypress) serves as the high-contrast accessible touchpoint for interactive text links and pressed states.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059` - Raw Champagne Gold)**: Reserved for prestige moments: editorial star ratings, certified artisan verifications, VIP tiers, and currency value accents.
- **Surfaces & Canvas**:
  - Global Canvas: `#FAF9F5` (Airy Linen)
  - Surface Subdued: `#F5F4EF` (Raw Alabaster)
  - Surface Pure / Card Container: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Chalk)
  - Surface Border / Hairline: `#E6E8E2` (Soft Thyme Hairline)
- **Typography & Neutrals**:
  - Dominant High-Emphasis Text: `#212822` (Charcoal Olive)
  - Secondary / Supportive Text: `#5F665C` (Muted Lichen)
  - Disabled / Hairline Muted Text: `#9DA399` (Stone Ash)

## Typography

The typographical pairing expresses heritage craftsmanship juxtaposed with modern digital clarity:

- **Display & Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`)**: Infuses editorial grandeur, high contrast serifs, and high-fashion poise. Applied to collection titles, editorial banners, hero introductions, and modal titles.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Imparts an open, warm, contemporary geometric humanist reading experience. Extremely legible in compact booking flows, ingredient lists, wellness rituals, and metadata tags.
- **Micro-labels and Overlines**: Always uppercase with tracked letter spacing (`+0.06em` to `+0.08em`) in `label-md` or `label-sm` to lend an organized, bespoke atelier quality to categories, tags, and product origins.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to a balanced 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (max structural width 1320px, auto-centered) and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Grid & Gutters**:
  - Desktop: 12 columns with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and `3rem` (`48px`) margins.
  - Tablet (768px – 1024px): 8 columns with `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) margins.
  - Mobile (<768px): 4 columns with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) outer canvas margins.
- **Rhythm & Padding**: Vertical spacing between sections uses spacious scale steps (`space-xl` to `space-2xl`), allowing hero imagery, artisanal photography, and serene typography breathing room. Inner component gaps consistently follow the `0.5rem` to `1.5rem` rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses low-contrast tonal layering and ultra-diffused, botanical-tinted shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#FAF9F5` (Airy Linen). Flat, non-elevated background tone.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` raised over the canvas through a hairline border of `1px solid #E6E8E2` coupled with an ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(33, 40, 34, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Floating Elements)**: Used for interactive product hover states, booking drawers, and floating bottom navigation: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(33, 40, 34, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays)**: Dialog surfaces rest on a tinted backdrop overlay of `rgba(33, 40, 34, 0.35)` with backdrop blur (`8px`), casting a soft, deep ambient glow: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(33, 40, 34, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Soft** shape language (`roundedness: 1`). 

Corners are gently smoothed (`0.25rem` / `4px` base) to balance architectural rigor with organic warmth:
- Small controls, inputs, and chips: `0.25rem` (4px).
- Cards, wellness treatment modules, and images: `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`).
- Modal sheets and primary dialog containers: `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / `12px`).
- Badges and pill action buttons (such as status pills or verification tags) optionally utilize circular caps (`9999px`) when distinguishing system metadata from structural content modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#9CAF88`, label `#FFFFFF` in `title-md`, `0.25rem` border radius, padding `14px 28px`. Hover shifts to `#899C74` with a subtle elevation shift.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Hairline border `1px solid #9CAF88`, background transparent, text `#586851`. Hover yields a soft wash of `rgba(156, 175, 136, 0.08)`.
- **Prestige / Gold Action**: Used for bespoke membership actions and concierge booking: `#C5A059` background with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Category Filter Chips**: `#FFFFFF` background with `1px solid #E6E8E2`, text `#5F665C`. Active state switches to `#9CAF88` text with `#9CAF88` border and a delicate `rgba(156, 175, 136, 0.12)` fill.
- **Luxury Rating & Verification**: Gold tinted pill (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.12)`) featuring `#C5A059` icon and typography in `label-sm`.

### Input Fields
- Understated elegance: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E6E8E2`, corner radius `4px`, padding `14px 16px`.
- Typography: `#212822` value text; `#9DA399` placeholder. Focus state transitions border to `#9CAF88` with an ambient glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(156, 175, 136, 0.15)`.

### Cards (Ritual & Product Showcases)
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E6E8E2`, corner radius `8px`, subtle shadow. Imagery inside cards maintains a natural 4:5 or 1:1 aspect ratio with gentle edge-to-edge top pinning and `16px` inner module padding for copy, pricing, and ratings.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square (`4px` radius) or circular control in `18px` size. Unchecked state: `1.5px solid #E6E8E2`, canvas `#FFFFFF`. Checked state: background `#9CAF88`, border `#9CAF88`, featuring an organic crisp chalk white icon.

### Bespoke Additions
- **Scent & Ingredient Accordion**: Delicate, hairline-divided horizontal drawers with smooth accordion motion, displaying Ethiopian highland botanicals (e.g., Frankincense, Nigella Sativa, Kosso).
- **Concierge Booking Bar**: A sticky, floating bottom dock for mobile views featuring subtle backdrop blur (`12px`), ivory fill (`rgba(250, 249, 245, 0.92)`), hairline top division, and dual quick-action booking triggers.
